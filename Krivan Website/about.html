About Krivan Industries Pvt. Ltd.

Nourishing Soil. Growing Tomorrow. Dedicated to building soil vitality through balanced organic nutrition and high-efficiency agricultural formulations.

Our Foundation

Committed to Sustainable Soil Fertility & High Crop Yield

Krivan Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a modern Indian agricultural enterprise specializing in organic soil conditioners, molasses-derived potash, phosphate rich organic manure (PROM), diatomite silicon granules, and chelated crop micronutrients.

Our core objective is to replace degraded soil conditions with nutrient-dense, biological environments. By maintaining optimal C:N ratios and supplying bio-available organic phosphorus, nitrogen, and potassium, our products restore long-term soil structure and enhance plant immunity against climatic stress.

Quality Assurance

Strict compliance with notified state grades and agricultural standards.

Farmer Centric

Formulations tailored for easy soil application and foliar uptake.

Our Mission

To provide farmers with scientifically formulated, organic carbon-enriched, and bio-available crop nutrients that eliminate soil contamination, improve water retention, and build resilient crop health across all soil conditions.

Our Vision

To be a trusted household name for agricultural quality in India, setting standard benchmarks for organic manure, molasses potash, silicon granules, and micronutrients while expanding robust partnerships with agricultural dealers and distributors.

Science & Innovation

Our Agricultural Philosophy

Soil health is the root of sustainable agricultural prosperity. Here is how our product lines contribute to agricultural success:

Soil Texture & Aeration

Our organic manures like KRIV-VASUNDHARA and KRIV-MRUDA improve physical soil aeration, water retention, and beneficial microbial activity.

Stress & Drought Resistance

KRIV-SILICA Diatomite granules strengthen plant cell walls, reducing transpiration loss and improving salinity and drought resistance.

Foliar Deficiency Correction

KRIV-LUSTER chelated micronutrients (Maharashtra Grade II) provide instant spray absorption to correct Zinc, Iron, Copper, Manganese, and Boron deficiencies.
